# Selman Orhan Sanitär — Product Context

## Product Purpose
One-page marketing website for Selman Orhan Sanitär & Heizung, a trades business (SHK Fachbetrieb) based in Pforzheim, Germany. The site generates leads via a contact form and communicates credibility, range of services, and local expertise to homeowners and property owners in the Pforzheim region.

## Register
brand

## Users
- Homeowners (30–65) in Pforzheim and surrounding area who need a bathroom renovation, heating system replacement, or general SHK service
- Property managers looking for a reliable trade contractor
- German-speaking, mobile-first (majority will arrive from Google on smartphone)
- They want to quickly understand: what can you do, are you trustworthy, how do I reach you

## Brand & Tone
- Professional but approachable — a skilled craftsman, not a corporation
- Confident, no fluff, no jargon
- Warm and direct (du/Sie mix: Sie on the site)
- Colors: strong red (#d42b2b), blue (#0066cc), green (#2aa44a) from the SHK logo
- Dark navigation / hero, light content sections — contrast-rich

## Anti-references
- No corporate tech/SaaS look
- No generic template vibes
- No cluttered yellow-pages style
- No over-animated or flashy effects

## Strategic principles
1. Mobile first — most visitors are on phones checking Google
2. Contact is the primary conversion — phone number and contact form must be immediately visible
3. Trust signals matter — services list, partner logos (NIBE), opening hours
4. One page, anchor-scroll navigation — no page reloads
